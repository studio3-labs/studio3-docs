# Rewards & Recognition

## What an Echo Actually Gets

<div class="arena-card">

<h3>💰 Two Different Things</h3>

<p><strong>Recognition</strong> is what you build by being right in public: an accuracy record, a calibration
record, and a progression title earned from verified outcomes.</p>

<p><strong>Rewards</strong> are real things - USDC and non-cash items - granted through Drops and Bounties, held
by the Arena and released on success.</p>

<p>Signalling and forecasting are free. Neither one costs you anything, and neither one is a way to
buy your way to a reward.</p>

</div>

!!! note "Being built, not yet live"
    Drops, Bounties, evidence submission and Arena-held rewards are settled decisions that are
    still being built. This page describes the model, not a feature you can use today.

## Recognition

### Your Accuracy Record

Every Forecast you make is scored against the verified outcome and added to a public record.

| Metric | What it measures |
|--------|------------------|
| **Accuracy** | How often you were on the right side of a settled question |
| **Calibration** | Whether your stated confidence matches how often you are right |
| **Coverage** | How many settled questions you actually committed to |
| **Earliness** | Whether you called it before it became obvious |

!!! tip "The hard number"
    Accuracy is easy to inflate by only forecasting the obvious. Calibration is not. If you say
    80% and you are right four times in five, your judgement is worth reading.

Signals are **not** scored. A Signal says what a venture should do, and preferences do not turn
out to be wrong.

### Progression Titles

Titles run **novice → adept → expert → master → legend**.

<div class="arena-card">

<h3>⭐ What Earns a Title</h3>

<ul>
<li><strong>Verified outcomes</strong> - contributions that were checked and counted</li>
<li><strong>Forecast accuracy</strong> - calls made in public, before the answer was known</li>
<li><strong>Delivered bounties</strong> - work claimed and completed</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>What does not earn a title:</strong> points, staking, holdings, activity volume, time served, or
anything you could buy. There is nothing to accumulate.</p>

</div>

A higher title does not make your Signal count for more. Every Echo's Signal weighs the same. What
it changes is how seriously people read your reasoning, and what the platform trusts you with.

## Rewards

### What a Reward Is

<div class="grid cards" markdown="1">
<div class="arena-card">

<h3>💵 USDC</h3>

<p>Real money, in a stablecoin. Attached to specific work or a specific milestone.</p>

</div>
<div class="arena-card">

<h3>🎁 Non-cash</h3>

<p>Merch, access, invitations, event tickets, credits, digital collectibles.</p>

</div>
</div>

### How Echoes Earn Them

**Bounties.** A specific piece of work posted with a reward attached. You claim it, deliver it,
the delivery is verified, and the reward is released. Delivered bounties count towards your title.

**Drops.** Rewards granted to participants around a venture, recognising contribution, early
support, or presence at a moment that mattered.

In both cases the reward is held by the **Arena** and released on verified success. Where several
people contributed, the Sender proposes how it is divided, contributors can dispute that, and an
Anchor arbitrates.

!!! info "Deliberately not stated here"
    The proportions by which a reward is split, and how custody of the funds is arranged, are not
    settled. Rather than guess, this guide says only what is decided.

### What Forecasting Does Not Earn

Being right does not, by itself, pay you. A correct forecast improves your record; it does not
release money. Rewards come from Drops and Bounties - from contribution, not from prediction.

The one exception is external: where a Polymarket prediction market exists on the same question
and you are eligible to use it, a forecast can carry money there. That is optional, external,
restricted by jurisdiction, and rare. Most milestones will never have one, and Studio3 works
completely without them.

## Consequences

<div class="arena-card">

<h3>🔥 Being Wrong in Public</h3>

<ul>
<li>A wrong forecast is <strong>recorded permanently</strong> against your accuracy</li>
<li>It <strong>costs you nothing else</strong> - there is nothing to lose because there was nothing to put up</li>
<li>Systematic overconfidence shows up in your calibration, where everyone can see it</li>
<li>Sitting out a question you do not understand costs you <strong>nothing at all</strong></li>
</ul>

</div>

That asymmetry is deliberate. The only thing at risk is the credibility of your judgement, which
is the only thing that should be.

## Getting Better

!!! tip "What actually improves your record"
    1. **Specialise.** Depth in a few domains beats thin coverage of many.

    2. **Write down your reasoning.** It is the only way to learn whether you were right for the right reason.

    3. **Commit early when you have an edge.** A call made before the crowd formed a view is worth far more than one made after.

    4. **Update when the evidence changes.** Changing your mind is good forecasting; never having committed is not.

    5. **Skip freely.** There is no reward for volume and no penalty for silence.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why it hurts |
|---------|--------------|
| **Forecasting everything** | Dilutes your record with questions you cannot judge |
| **Only forecasting the obvious** | Good accuracy, worthless calibration |
| **Copying the consensus** | Teaches you nothing and calibrates you badly |
| **Signalling your forecast** | Confuses what you want with what you expect |
| **Chasing a reward** | Rewards come from contribution, not from being right |
