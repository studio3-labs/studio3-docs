# Anchor Compensation

## Being Paid for Judgement

<div class="arena-card">

<h3>💰 The Short Version</h3>

<p>Anchors are compensated in <strong>real rewards</strong> - USDC and non-cash items - released by the Arena
when the work they verified is confirmed as delivered. There is no Studio3 token, nothing to
stake, and no yield to farm.</p>

</div>

!!! note "Being built, not yet live"

    today.

## What Is Settled

### Rewards Are Real

Compensation is **USDC**, plus non-cash items where a venture or studio offers them - access,
credits, tickets, merch, digital collectibles. It is not paid in a platform token, because there
isn't one.

### The Arena Holds It

The reward attached to a milestone is committed to the **Arena** before the work starts, and
released when the outcome is verified. Anchors are paid out of what the Arena releases, not out
of an emissions schedule.

### Verification Comes First, and Not Immediately

At launch, **Studio3 staff verify** milestone completion and bounty delivery.
**Anchors take verification over** as the platform matures. Until that handover, an Anchor's
contribution is mentorship, guidance, arbitration and standards work rather than formal
verification.

### How a Reward Is Divided

When a milestone is delivered by several contributors, the **Sender proposes the split**,
**contributors can dispute it**, and an **Anchor arbitrates** the dispute.

## What Is Not Settled

!!! warning "Deliberately not specified here"
    The following are genuinely undecided, and this guide will not invent numbers for them:

    - **Rates.** What an Anchor is paid per verification, per arbitration, or per engagement.
    - **Proportions.** What share of a milestone's reward goes to Anchors as against Senders,
      Echoes and other contributors.
    - **Custody.** How the funds an Arena holds are actually held and released.

    Anyone quoting a percentage for Anchor compensation is quoting something that has not been
    decided.

!!! danger "Conflicts of interest"
    An Anchor who is paid directly by a venture cannot also be the neutral party verifying that
    venture's milestones or arbitrating its disputes. Declare the relationship and step back from
    the decision.

## What Actually Grows Your Standing

Anchor progression titles - novice, adept, expert, master, legend - come from
**verified outcomes, accurate judgement, and delivered work**.
They do not come from points, staking, holdings, or time served.

What builds an Anchor's standing:

| Input | Why it counts |
|-------|---------------|
| **Verifications that hold up** | Decisions that survive challenge and review |
| **Founders who improved** | Ventures measurably better for your guidance |
| **Arbitration accepted by both sides** | The hardest thing in the role |
| **Standards work** | Rubrics and precedents others use |

What damages it: verification that is later overturned, bias, negligence, and any relationship
you failed to declare.

## Planning Your Involvement

!!! tip "Practical advice"
    Because rates are not yet set, treat Anchor work as something you do alongside your main
    income, not instead of it. The [Requirements](requirements.md) page asks you not to be
    financially dependent on this role, and that remains the right posture until compensation is
    defined.

## Tax

Rewards you receive are income. How they are classified depends on your jurisdiction and on
whether the reward is cash or in kind. Keep records of what you received, when, and for what
work, and take professional advice.

## FAQ

**Q: How much does an Anchor earn per validation?**
A: Not yet decided. Any number you have seen is not authoritative.

**Q: Do I need to put up money to become an Anchor?**
A: No. There is no stake, no bond, and no token to hold.

**Q: Can my Anchor standing be slashed?**
A: There is nothing to slash. Poor or overturned verification affects your standing, your future
assignments, and ultimately whether you keep the role.

**Q: Am I paid if a milestone fails?**
A: The reward attached to that milestone is not released. How Anchor work is compensated
independently of milestone outcome is part of what is still being decided.
